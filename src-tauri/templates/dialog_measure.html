A capacity needs the discharge current it was measured at, and any reading needs the instrument it came from. Both are refused without them, because a figure that does not say how it was produced cannot be compared with the next one and is worse than no figure at all.

A purchase records only the date this cell entered service, so there is nothing else to fill in.
